## Make a MakeCode Tutorial!

# Introduction

Welcome to Sensei Klyde's First MakeCode Arcade Tutorial!!

This is a step-by-step guide on to how to complete the first ever project on IMPACT, White Belt 1-1!

## Step 1 / 5 : Set a Background Color!

### "To reach the peak of the mountain, one must first begin at the bottom."

- :tree: Add the first block that will start your climb on IMPACT! Navigate to your Toolbox, and find the ``||scene:set background color||`` block to your ``||loops: on start||`` container!

- :mouse pointer: Click the grey bubble **inside of** the ``||scene: set background color to||`` block to **pick a color**!

Check your code out on the Simulator! Your background should have turned into the color you chose!

## Step 2 / 5 : Add and Choose a Sprite!

It's time to make your still image into a portrait!
Let's create a Sprite!

### Sprites are any visible object in your video game!

- :sprites: In your Toolbox, open the ``||sprites:Sprites||`` tab and select the ``||sprites: set mySprite to [image]||`` block and drag it into your ``||loops: on start||`` container.

- :logic: Click the grey square **inside** your coding block to draw your sprite! If you can't find the inspiration to draw your own characters, have a look at our **Gallery** tab above the canvas! 

## Step 3 / 5 : Choose a position!

You've probably noticed that your sprite is in the **middle** of your screen! 

That's because when a sprite is created without a position, it defaults to the middle.

### Add a Position for Your Sprite!

- :tree: Click in the ``||sprites: Sprites||`` tab and select the ``||sprites: set mySprite position to [x 0] [y 0]||`` block and drag it into your ``||loops: on start||`` container.

- :mouse pointer: Now click either the **x 0** bubble or the **y 0** bubble to open the 'Position Selector' menu.

- :mouse pointer: It's up to you to decide the position of your sprite, but make sure it **stays in the screen**!
