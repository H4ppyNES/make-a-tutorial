## Make a MakeCode Tutorial!

# Introduction

Welcome to Sensei Klyde's First MakeCode Arcade Tutorial!!

This is a step-by-step guide on to how to complete the first ever project on IMPACT, White Belt 1-1!

## Step 1 / 5 : Set a Background Color!

### "To reach the peak of the mountain, one must first begin at the bottom."

Add the first block that will start your climb on IMPACT! 

- :tree: Navigate to your Toolbox, and find the ``||scene:set background color||`` block to your ``||loops: on start||`` container!

- :mouse pointer: Click the grey bubble **inside of** the ``||scene: set background color||`` block to **pick a color**!

Check your code out on the Simulator! Your background should have turned into the color you chose!

## Step 2 / 5 : Add and Choose a Sprite!

It's time to make your still image into a portrait!
Let's create a Sprite!

### Sprites are any visible object in your video game!

- :paper plane: In your Toolbox, open the ``||sprites:Sprites||`` tab and select the ``||sprites: set mySprite to [image]||`` block and drag it into your ``||loops: on start||`` container.

- :mouse pointer: Click the grey square **inside** your coding block to draw your sprite! If you can't find the inspiration to draw your own characters, have a look at our **Gallery** tab above the canvas! 

## Step 3 / 5 : Choose a position!

You've probably noticed that your sprite is in the **middle** of your screen! 

That's because when a sprite is created without a position, it **defaults to the middle**.

### Add a Position for Your Sprite!

- :paper plane: In the ``||sprites: Sprites||`` tab, select the ``||sprites: set mySprite position to [x 0] [y 0]||`` block and drag it into your ``||loops: on start||`` container.

- :mouse pointer: Now click either the **[x 0]** bubble or the **[y 0]** bubble to open the **'Position Selector'** menu.

It's up to you to decide the position of your sprite, but make sure it **stays in the screen**!

## Step 4 / 5 : Make The Sprite Say Something!

Let's make the sprite say something to the players of your game! 

### Find the Say Block!

- :paper plane: In the ``||sprites: Sprites||`` tab, look for the ``||sprites: mySprite say :)||`` block and add it into your ``||loops: on start||`` container.

- :mouse pointer: In the ``||sprites: mySprite say :)||`` block, replace **:)** with what you want your sprite to say!

*Hint: If you have an animal sprite, maybe make it say the sound they make?*

## Step 5 / 5 : Ninja See, Ninja Do!

The previous steps have taught you how to create, position, and command a sprite.

Now, you are instructed to **create a second sprite** that will be positioned away from the **first sprite** and programmed to say something!

### Replicate and Re-create!

- :info: You'll be using both the ``||scene:Scene||`` menu and the ``||sprites:Sprites||`` menu to make it happen!

- :mouse pointer: Make sure to replace the **x [0]**, **y [0]**, and the **:)** bubbles to fully customise what how your second sprite is going to act!

When you're done, raise your hand and call a **Code Sensei** to verify your work and assign you an additional challenge!
