Welcome to Sensei Klyde's First MakeCode Arcade Tutorial!!

This is a step-by-step guide on to how to complete the first ever project on IMPACT, White Belt 1-1!

## Step 1 / 5 : Set a Background Color!

### This is to test the timeline fortnite skibidi toielt

- :tree: Add the first block that will start your climb on IMPACT! Navigate to your Toolbox, and find the ``||scene:set background color||`` block to your ``||loops: on start||`` container!

- :mouse pointer: Click the grey bubble **inside of** the ``||scene: set background color to||`` block to **pick a color**!

Check your code out on the Simulator! Your background should have turned into the color you chose!

## Step 2 / 5 : Add and Choose a Sprite!

It's time to make your still image into a portrait!

### Let's create a Sprite! Sprites are any visible object in your video game!

- :sprites: In your Toolbox, open the ``||sprites:Sprites||`` tab and select the ``||sprites: set mySprite to [image]||`` block and drag it into your ``||loops: on start||`` container.
